Bun

Our Heritage

Where Every Cup
Has a Story

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee — where the first coffee plants grew wild in the Kaffa highlands over a thousand years ago. Bun, the Amharic word for coffee, is at the heart of Ethiopian culture, ceremony, and daily life.

We bring that heritage to Mombasa — authentic, uncompromised, and deeply rooted in tradition. Every product we offer is a direct connection to that origin.

"Coffee is our bread. It is our identity. It is our gift to the world."

— Ethiopian Coffee Tradition
